Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are

Valley Advertising FZE (“Valley Advertising”, “we”, “us”) is a branding and advertising manufacturer based in Sharjah, UAE. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this website or contact us.

Information we collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to answer your enquiry.

  • Details you send us — your name, phone number, email address, company and project details when you call, WhatsApp, email or otherwise contact us.
  • Project files — artwork, logos and photos you share so we can prepare a quote or produce your order.

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or user accounts, and it does not ask you to create a profile.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotations
  • To produce, deliver and install your order
  • To send order updates and invoices
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

Third-party services

Some pages load content from other providers, such as an embedded Google Map on the Contact page, and links to WhatsApp and Instagram. When you use these, the provider may collect technical data (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with people who need it to fulfil your order (for example delivery or installation partners), or where the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry and order records for as long as needed for the purposes above and for accounting requirements, and take reasonable steps to protect them from loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
