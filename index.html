About Us

Welcome to VALLEY ADVERTISING — Sharjah's in-house branding manufacturer and your partner in custom branding solutions across the UAE.

At Valley Advertising, we specialise in delivering premium, tailor-made branding and advertising products that help businesses stand out. From eye-catching advertising flags and branded tents to top-quality display stands, custom signage, exhibition kiosks, and acrylic awards, we bring your brand to life through physical visibility and professional presentation.

Valley Advertising FZE is a UAE-based branding, signage, and advertising manufacturing company specialising in custom solutions for corporate clients, retailers, event organisers, government organisations, and marketing agencies. Unlike trading companies, we operate our own manufacturing facility at SRTIP, Sharjah — which gives us complete control over quality, customisation, and delivery timelines without outsourcing to third-party factories.

Every product we manufacture is built for UAE conditions. Summer temperatures above 45°C, constant direct sunlight, coastal humidity — our material choices reflect this reality. We use UV-resistant polyester fabrics for flags, powder-coated mild steel for structural products, and cast vinyl rated for vehicle exterior use in extreme heat. These aren't incidental choices — they're the result of five years of learning what holds up in this climate and what doesn't.

We've worked across retail, hospitality, healthcare, real estate, government, education, events, and corporate sectors. Whether your brief calls for 100 feather flags for a roadside campaign in Dubai or a single bespoke acrylic award for a corporate gala in Sharjah — the same quality and professionalism goes into every order.

When you work with Valley Advertising, you're dealing directly with the manufacturer. Your questions get answered faster. Changes get implemented without a chain of subcontractors. Production can be tracked from first cut to final handover. And the pricing reflects material and skill, not layers of intermediary margin.

We serve clients across Dubai, Sharjah, and the wider UAE with end-to-end solutions — from design and fabrication to delivery and on-site installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our branding products, delivery, and services across UAE.

Where is Valley Advertising located in UAE?

Valley Advertising is based in SRTIP, Sharjah, UAE, where we operate our own in-house manufacturing facility. We deliver and install products across Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, and all UAE emirates.

What branding products does Valley Advertising manufacture?

We produce 50+ custom branding products including advertising flags (feather, teardrop, hoisting, bridge), branded tents, roll-up banners, display stands, exhibition kiosks, custom signage, acrylic awards, vehicle wraps, vinyl stickers, and corporate promotional gifts — all manufactured in-house in Sharjah, UAE.

How quickly can Valley Advertising deliver branding products in UAE?

Most standard products are delivered within 3–5 business days from artwork approval. Rush 24–48 hour production is available for urgent requirements. We deliver to all UAE emirates including Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, RAK, and Fujairah.

Does Valley Advertising offer installation services?

Yes. Our team provides professional delivery and on-site installation for flags, tents, kiosks, signage, and display stands across Dubai, Sharjah, and the wider UAE. Contact us for site-specific installation pricing.

Do you offer design services with product orders?

Yes. Our in-house team can develop original artwork from your brand brief, adapt existing brand guidelines to product templates, or prepare print-ready files from your supplied design. Ask us about design services when you enquire about your order.
