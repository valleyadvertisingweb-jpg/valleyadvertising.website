Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our advertising products, materials, and ordering process.

What types of advertising flags does Valley Advertising manufacture?

We manufacture feather flags, teardrop flags, L-banner flags, 4mtr curve flags, 6mtr advertising flags, 8mtr hoisting flags, bridge flags, table flags, conference pole flags, and terrace flags — all with full custom printing of your brand artwork in UV-resistant inks.

What sizes of branding tents are available?

Standard sizes are 3×3m and 4×4m. Custom marquee sizes and larger commercial structures are available to order. All tents feature custom-printed canopies with your logo and brand colours. We also manufacture branded café umbrellas and canopy side walls for complete outdoor branding coverage.

Can you build custom exhibition booths for trade shows in Dubai?

Yes. Valley Advertising designs and fabricates custom exhibition booths for trade shows and corporate events including Dubai World Trade Centre events. We handle design, fabrication, transport, installation, and dismantling — a complete end-to-end service for exhibitors across UAE.

Do your outdoor advertising products come with a warranty?

All our products are manufactured with UAE climate in mind. Flag fabric typically lasts 12–18 months in outdoor use with standard care. Metal and structural products carry quality assurance on workmanship. Vinyl graphics carry a minimum 2-year outdoor durability rating.

What is the minimum order quantity for flags and branding products?

Valley Advertising accepts orders from a single unit for most products. There is no minimum order requirement. For bulk orders of 10 or more units, we offer volume pricing. Ask us for a bulk quote when you enquire — we provide discounts for large orders and repeat clients.
