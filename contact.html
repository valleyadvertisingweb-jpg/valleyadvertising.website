Why Choose Valley Advertising

Direct from Manufacturer — No Middlemen

When you contact Valley Advertising, you're reaching the manufacturer directly — not a sales team that routes your brief to a third-party factory. The person handling your enquiry is the same team that will produce your product. That means real answers, faster decisions, and a project that stays on track from first contact to final installation.

We respond to all quote requests within 24 hours on business days. For urgent requirements, WhatsApp is the fastest channel — typically same business day response. Once we understand your brief, we'll send a detailed quote covering product specs, material options, production lead time, delivery cost, and installation pricing where applicable.

We work with any level of brief. You can come to us with a complete print-ready file and specific product specification — or with nothing more than "I need flags for next week." Both conversations are fine. Our team will help you arrive at the right product for your budget, timeline, and visual goal. No commitment required to request a quote.

Valley Advertising serves clients across all UAE emirates from our manufacturing base in SRTIP, Sharjah. Client facility visits are welcome — call or WhatsApp ahead to schedule, and our team will walk you through the production facility and discuss your project in person.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting a quote and working with Valley Advertising.

How do I get a quote from Valley Advertising?

Fill in the Quick Quote Request form above, WhatsApp us at +971-58-199-3786, call 058-1993786, or email info@valleyadvertisinguae.com. We respond within 24 hours on business days. For urgent requirements, WhatsApp is the fastest channel — typically same business day response.

Can I visit the Valley Advertising manufacturing facility in Sharjah?

Yes. Our facility is at SRTIP, Sharjah, UAE. We welcome client visits — WhatsApp or call ahead to schedule so our team can show you the production facility and discuss your project requirements in person.

What is the minimum order quantity for advertising flags?

Valley Advertising accepts orders from a single unit. There is no minimum order requirement for most products. For bulk orders of 10 or more units, we offer volume pricing — ask for a bulk quote when you enquire.

Does Valley Advertising offer free design services?

Design consultation is included with every order. If you have existing brand guidelines or artwork, we adapt it to your product. If you need original design from scratch, our team can develop concepts — design fees may apply depending on complexity. Ask us when you enquire.

Does Valley Advertising ship to other GCC countries?

Our primary service area is within the UAE. For export to GCC countries including Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, and Qatar, contact us directly and we will discuss logistics and pricing based on your destination and order size.
