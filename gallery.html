Questions About Our Portfolio

How to use this gallery and get more details about any project you see.

Can I request more details about a specific project in the gallery?

Yes. Contact us with a description or screenshot of the project you're interested in and we'll share further details — including exact dimensions, materials used, production timeline, and pricing for a similar order.

Are the gallery images real projects or stock photography?

All images in this gallery are real projects produced and installed by Valley Advertising's team. We photograph our work at client sites and in our Sharjah facility before handover. No stock photography is used.

Can Valley Advertising replicate a style I've seen elsewhere?

Yes. If you have a reference image — from our gallery, another supplier, or anywhere online — share it with us when you enquire. Our design team will interpret it and propose how we can achieve the same look using our materials and production methods.

How frequently is the gallery updated?

The gallery is updated regularly as new projects are completed. We photograph most major installations and add them to the gallery within a few weeks of project completion. Follow us on Instagram @valleyadvertising for the most up-to-date project photos.
