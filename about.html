Who We Are

Built from Passion for Branding

Valley Advertising FZE is a UAE-based branding, signage, and advertising manufacturing company specialising in custom branding solutions for businesses across the UAE. We manufacture and supply high-quality advertising products for corporate clients, retailers, event organisers, government organisations, and marketing agencies.

Unlike trading companies, we operate our own manufacturing facility at SRTIP, Sharjah — which gives us complete control over quality, customisation, and delivery timelines. Every product is manufactured entirely in-house with no outsourcing, enabling us to maintain strict quality standards and meet tight project deadlines.

We provide complete branding solutions from design through manufacturing and installation, serving clients across Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain, and throughout the UAE.

How We Work

In-House Manufacturing — Full Control, Zero Outsourcing

When you place an order with Valley Advertising, you're activating a process that runs entirely through our Sharjah facility. Raw materials — fabric rolls, aluminium profiles, MDF boards, vinyl films, steel stock — are sourced directly from UAE and international suppliers. Our team then processes, cuts, prints, welds, routes, and finishes every component in-house. Nothing leaves without a quality inspection.

This in-house control has a direct practical impact: last-minute changes can be accommodated without restarting with a new subcontractor. Progress photos can be shared at any stage of production. When something needs adjusting, it's corrected the same day — not after a week of back-and-forth with an external supplier.

Our pricing reflects material, labour, and skill — not layers of intermediary margin. That's the practical value of dealing directly with the manufacturer.

Common Questions About Valley Advertising

Quick answers about our company, manufacturing, and experience in UAE branding.

When was Valley Advertising founded?

Valley Advertising was founded in 2019 in Sharjah, UAE. We have completed 500+ projects over more than 5 years of operation, growing from a solo-founder enterprise to a team serving clients across the UAE.

Does Valley Advertising have its own manufacturing facility?

Yes. Our manufacturing facility is located in SRTIP, Sharjah, UAE. All products — flags, tents, kiosks, signage, acrylic pieces, and fabricated structures — are produced entirely in-house, giving us direct quality control from raw material to finished product with no outsourcing.

What industries has Valley Advertising served in UAE?

We have delivered branding projects for retail chains, hospitality groups, real estate developers, government campaigns, healthcare facilities, universities, events companies, and corporate clients across Dubai, Sharjah, and the wider UAE. Our experience spans outdoor advertising, exhibition branding, retail environments, and corporate gifting.

How does Valley Advertising ensure product quality?

Because we operate our own in-house manufacturing facility at SRTIP, Sharjah, we maintain quality control at every stage — from material sourcing through production, finishing, and pre-delivery inspection. Nothing leaves our facility without passing quality review. No outsourcing, no third-party delays, no quality ambiguity.

Can Valley Advertising handle both small and large orders?

Yes. We work with orders of any scale — from a single custom trophy to a nationwide outdoor campaign requiring 200+ flags. Our in-house manufacturing capacity allows us to handle both without compromising on quality, lead time, or attention to detail. The same professionalism applies to every order.
