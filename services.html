Custom Branding Services UAE

From concept and design to manufacturing, delivery and installation — we handle every step of your branding journey.

Creative Design In-House Manufacturing Delivery & Installation

Our Branding Services

Valley Advertising FZE delivers specialist branding and fabrication services from our own manufacturing facility at SRTIP, Sharjah. From advertising flags, custom tents, and display stands to metal fabrication, carpentry, acrylic, vinyl printing, and vehicle branding — we manage every step from design through installation across all UAE emirates.

Flags & Bases for Rental

Advertising flags are one of the highest-impact, most cost-effective tools for outdoor brand visibility in the UAE. We manufacture and rent feather flags, teardrop flags, hoisting flags, L-banners, and bridge flags in heights from 2 to 8 metres — complete with the right base for your surface type (grass, sand, or concrete). All flags use UV-resistant polyester fabric with colour-fast inks built for UAE sunlight. Delivery, installation, and collection handled by our team across Dubai and Sharjah.

Metal Fabrication

Our metal fabrication services deliver precision-engineered solutions for commercial, retail, exhibition, signage, and architectural applications. We specialise in cutting, welding, bending, drilling, grinding, polishing, powder coating, and finishing — working with mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, and galvanised steel to manufacture display frames, signage structures, exhibition stands, retail fixtures, brackets, kiosk frames, and decorative metalwork. All welding, grinding, and powder coating runs in-house at our SRTIP, Sharjah facility. From design development and material selection to fabrication and installation, we ensure every project meets the highest standards of strength and finish quality. Serving Dubai, Sharjah, and all UAE.

Carpentry Works

Our carpentry services provide customised woodwork solutions for retail, commercial, exhibition, hospitality, and office environments. We specialise in retail interiors, exhibition stands, office furniture, reception counters, display units, kiosks, cabinets, wall panels, shelving systems, and customised wooden structures. We work with MDF, plywood, veneer, laminates, and solid wood — using CNC routing for precision edges and traditional joinery for structural strength. From concept and design to fabrication, finishing, and installation, we deliver precision workmanship that enhances the look and functionality of any space. Available across Dubai, Sharjah, and all UAE.

Acrylic Fabrication

Our acrylic fabrication services provide innovative customised solutions for branding, retail displays, exhibitions, and promotional applications. We create display boxes, product holders, brochure holders, menu stands, signage panels, tabletop displays, retail fixtures, protective covers, and custom acrylic structures — in clear, frosted, coloured, and custom finishes. Using advanced cutting, bending, polishing, laser engraving, and CNC routing, we produce high-precision acrylic products with a premium finish. Whether you need a single corporate award or a full retail display solution, our team delivers durable and visually refined acrylic fabrications. Turnaround 3–7 days. Sharjah-based, serving all UAE.

Vinyl Printing

Our vinyl printing services deliver high-quality graphics for businesses, retail stores, exhibitions, offices, vehicles, and commercial spaces. We specialise in custom vinyl printing for shopfronts, window graphics, wall graphics, floor graphics, vehicle wraps, promotional stickers, branding decals, signboards, and exhibition displays. Using advanced large-format printing technology and premium self-adhesive vinyl, we produce vibrant, high-resolution, durable prints for both indoor and outdoor applications. Products include wall graphics, window stickers, floor graphics (anti-slip), safety stickers, branding stickers, and custom vinyl. Professional installation included. Serving all UAE emirates.

Vehicle Branding

Turn your vehicles into powerful mobile advertisements with our professional vehicle branding and wrapping solutions. We provide custom branding for cars, vans, buses, trucks, commercial fleets, and corporate vehicles using premium-quality vinyl graphics and high-resolution printing. Services include full vehicle wraps, partial wraps, fleet branding, promotional graphics, reflective decals, window graphics, and custom vehicle stickers. We use cast vinyl rated for UAE outdoor conditions: heat-resistant, bubble-free, and built to survive car-wash cycles. Design, printing, and professional installation included. Fleet discounts available for 3+ vehicles. Serving Dubai, Sharjah, and all UAE.

Common Questions About Our Services

Everything you need to know about branding services, timelines, and installation in UAE.

How long does it take to manufacture advertising flags in UAE?

Standard advertising flags take 3–5 business days from artwork approval. Urgent orders can be completed in 24–48 hours depending on quantity and type. Valley Advertising produces all flags in-house at our Sharjah facility, giving us direct control over production timelines.

Do you offer installation of flags and signage across UAE?

Yes. Valley Advertising provides delivery and professional installation across Dubai, Sharjah, and the wider UAE. Our team handles site surveys, assembly, positioning, and dismantling at the end of your campaign. Contact us for site-specific installation pricing.

What materials do you use for outdoor advertising products in UAE?

For UAE outdoor conditions, we use UV-resistant polyester fabrics for flags, powder-coated aluminium and mild steel for structural products, marine-grade materials for outdoor carpentry, and cast vinyl rated for vehicle exterior use and temperatures up to 50°C.

Can you design vehicle wraps if we only have a logo?

Yes. Our in-house design team creates full vehicle branding concepts from minimal brief — just your logo, brand colours, and contact details. We provide digital mockups on your specific vehicle model before production starts. No print until you approve.

Do you serve clients outside Sharjah?

Yes. While our manufacturing facility is in SRTIP, Sharjah, we deliver and install projects across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, RAK, Fujairah, and all UAE emirates. Contact us with your location for delivery and installation pricing.
